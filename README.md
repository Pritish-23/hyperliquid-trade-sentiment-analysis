# Hyperliquid Trader Behavior vs Market Sentiment Analysis

## Project Objective

Analyze how market sentiment (Fear & Greed Index) affects trader behavior and performance on Hyperliquid.

---

## Datasets Used

### 1. Fear & Greed Dataset
- Date
- Classification
- Sentiment Value

### 2. Hyperliquid Historical Trades
- Account
- PnL
- Trade Size
- Side
- Timestamp
- Coin
- etc.

1. Data Cleaning

4. Feature Engineering

8. Strategy Recommendations

---

## Key Features Engineered

- Win Rate
- Daily PnL
- Trade Frequency
- Average Trade Size
- Trader Segments

---

## Key Insights

- Extreme Greed conditions produced the highest average profitability and win rates.
- Fear markets showed larger average trade sizes and higher volatility.
- Infrequent traders achieved higher average profitability per trade.
- Frequent traders had higher win rates but lower profit efficiency.

---

## Strategy Recommendations

### 1. Selective Trading During Greed
Focus on high-conviction setups during bullish sentiment instead of excessive trading.

### 2. Reduce Exposure During Fear
Use smaller position sizes and stricter risk management during fearful markets.

### 3. Avoid Overtrading
Higher trade frequency does not necessarily improve profitability efficiency.
